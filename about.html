EXPERIENCE

My Learning Journey

When I studied in the PPLG major at school, I started learning web development in my class. At that time, I was a beginner who did not understand how to create a website. My teachers guided me, and I also learned from online tutorials and practice at home.

I learned basic coding such as HTML and CSS step by step. At the beginning, I felt confused because everything was new to me. I did not know how to construct a webpage or design it properly.

However, I kept learning and practicing regularly. Slowly, I started to understand how to create layouts and design simple web pages.

During the process, I faced several challenges. One of the main problems was creating a moving navbar. I found it difficult to make the navbar responsive and smooth when scrolling.

Another challenge was creating a laptop animation that could move properly on the screen. Sometimes the animation did not work as I expected and the position was not correct.

To solve these problems, I searched for solutions on the internet, watched video tutorials, and read documentation. I tried different coding methods and practiced many times.

I also learned from my mistakes and kept improving my code. After several attempts, I finally understood how to use CSS animations and a little JavaScript to make the navbar and animation work properly.

From this experience, I learned that web development requires patience, practice, and consistency. Even though I faced difficulties at the beginning, I did not give up and continued learning.

This experience motivates me to keep improving my skills in web development in the future.
